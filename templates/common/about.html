About

Cloud Storage is a way to store digital data online, using remote servers maintained by a third-party provider, rather than on a local device like a hard drive. It allows users to access their files from any internet-connected device, offering flexibility and scalability. Think of it as renting storage space on someone else's computer network, rather than owning and managing your own.
